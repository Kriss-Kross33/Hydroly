# Individual Developer Setup Guide

## Quick Start for Individual Developers

As an individual developer (not a business), you have a **simpler and faster** setup process! This guide is specifically for you.

---

## ✅ What You Can Do as an Individual

- ✅ Set up merchant accounts on both platforms
- ✅ Use your personal name (no business name needed)
- ✅ Use your personal bank account
- ✅ Use your SSN (US) or tax ID
- ✅ **NO D-U-N-S number needed** (iOS) - saves 1-2 weeks!
- ✅ Faster approval process

---

## 🤖 Android - Google Play Console (Individual)

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Go to Google Play Console**
   - Navigate to **Monetize** → **Subscriptions**
   - Click **"Set up payments profile"**

2. **Provide Personal Information**:
   - **Name**: Your legal name (first and last)
   - **Address**: Your personal address in Ghana
   - **Phone**: Your Ghana phone number (include country code +233)
   - **Email**: Your email address
   - **Country**: Ghana

3. **Tax Information**:
   - **Ghana Individual**: Your **Ghana Tax Identification Number (TIN)**
     - If you don't have a TIN, you can get one from the Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA)
     - Visit: [GRA TIN Registration](https://www.gra.gov.gh/tin-registration/)
   - Complete tax forms for Ghana
   - Google will provide appropriate tax forms for Ghana

4. **Bank Account**:
   - **You can use your personal Ghanaian bank account**
   - Bank name (e.g., GCB Bank, Ecobank, Standard Chartered, etc.)
   - Account number
   - **SWIFT/BIC code** (required for international transfers)
     - Example: GCB Bank = GCBGGHAC
     - Example: Ecobank = ECOCGHAC
     - Example: Standard Chartered = SCBLGHAC
   - Account holder name: **Your name** (must match the name above)
   - **Currency**: Your account currency (GHS or USD if you have a USD account)

5. **Submit and Wait**:
   - Review all information
   - Submit for approval
   - **Timeline**: 1-3 business days typically

### What You'll Need (Ghana)

- ✅ **Ghana TIN** (Tax Identification Number) - Get from GRA if needed
- ✅ Personal Ghanaian bank account details
- ✅ Bank SWIFT/BIC code
- ✅ Proof of address (may be requested)
- ✅ Government-issued ID (Ghana National ID or Passport)

## 📱 iOS - App Store Connect (Individual)

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Go to App Store Connect**
   - Click your name → **Agreements, Tax, and Banking**
   - Click **Request Agreement** for Paid Applications

2. **Accept Paid Applications Agreement**:
   - Read the agreement
   - Accept on behalf of yourself (as an individual)
   - Click **Submit**

3. **Complete Banking Information**:
   - Click **Add Bank Account**
   - **You can use your personal bank account**
   - Provide:
     - Bank name
     - Account number
     - Routing number (US) or SWIFT code
     - Account holder name: **Your name**
   - Verify test deposits (Apple will make small deposits)

4. **Complete Tax Information**:
   - Select your country (e.g., **Ghana**)
   - Complete tax forms for your country:
     - **US**: W-9 form with SSN
     - **Ghana**: Tax forms with Ghana TIN
     - **Other countries**: Country-specific tax forms
   - Provide:
     - Name: **Your legal name**
     - Tax ID: **Your country's tax ID** (SSN for US, TIN for Ghana, etc.)
     - Address: **Your address**
     - Tax classification: **Individual**
   - Submit tax form

5. **Complete Business Information**:
   - Navigate to **Business Information**
   - **Select "Individual"** (not "Business")
   - Provide:
     - **Legal Name**: Your full name
     - **Address**: Your personal address
     - **Phone**: Your phone number
     - **Email**: Your email
   - **NO D-U-N-S Number needed!** ✅
   - Save information

6. **Wait for Approval**:
   - **Timeline**: 1-3 business days (much faster than businesses!)
   - Check status in **Agreements, Tax, and Banking**
   - You'll receive email updates

### What You'll Need

- ✅ Your SSN (US) or tax ID
- ✅ Personal bank account details
- ✅ Government-issued ID (may be requested)
- ✅ **NO D-U-N-S number** (this is the advantage!)

## 🚨 Common Questions

### Q: Can I use my personal bank account?
**A**: Yes! Both platforms allow personal bank accounts for individual developers.

### Q: Do I need to register a business?
**A**: No! You can operate as an individual developer. You can always convert to a business later if needed.

### Q: Will I get a 1099 form?
**A**: Yes, both platforms will send you tax forms (1099-NEC for US) if you earn above the threshold.

### Q: Can I change from individual to business later?
**A**: Yes, but it requires updating your information and may need D-U-N-S number. It's easier to start as individual if you're not sure.

### Q: What if I want to use a business name later?
**A**: You can update your information, but you'll need to provide business documents and D-U-N-S number at that time.

### Q: Is there any disadvantage to being an individual?
**A**: No major disadvantages. You might want to consider:
- **Tax implications**: Consult a tax professional or GRA about how to handle income
- **Liability**: Consider liability protection if you're concerned
- **Professional appearance**: Some users might prefer seeing a business name

### Q: I'm from Ghana - what do I need?
**A**: For Ghanaian developers:
- ✅ **Ghana TIN** (Tax Identification Number) - Required
- ✅ Personal Ghanaian bank account with SWIFT code
- ✅ Government-issued ID (National ID or Passport)
- ✅ Proof of address in Ghana
- ✅ NO D-U-N-S number needed (individual developers)

### Q: What bank SWIFT code do I use?
**A**: Common Ghanaian bank SWIFT codes:
- **GCB Bank**: GCBGGHAC
- **Ecobank**: ECOCGHAC
- **Standard Chartered**: SCBLGHAC
- **Absa Bank**: BARCGHAC
- **Fidelity Bank**: FBLIGHAC
- **Stanbic Bank**: SBICGHAC
- **CalBank**: CALBGHAC
- Check with your specific bank if unsure

## 💰 Tax Considerations (Ghana)

As an individual developer receiving subscription revenue from Ghana:

1. **Ghana Tax Requirements**:
   - **Ghana TIN Required**: You need a Tax Identification Number (TIN)
     - Get your TIN from [Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA)](https://www.gra.gov.gh/tin-registration/)
     - Free to obtain
     - Required for receiving international payments
   
   - **Income Tax**: 
     - Report subscription income to GRA
     - May be subject to personal income tax
     - Consult GRA or tax professional for rates
   
   - **Withholding Tax**:
     - Apple/Google may withhold taxes depending on agreements
     - Check your tax treaty status with US (if applicable)

2. **Bank Account Considerations**:
   - **Currency**: 
     - If you have a USD account, payments can be received in USD
     - If GHS account only, bank will convert USD to GHS
     - Check your bank's conversion rates and fees
   
   - **International Transfers**:
     - Ensure your bank accepts international SWIFT transfers
     - Some banks may require additional documentation
     - Check minimum balance requirements

3. **Recommendation**:
   - ✅ Get your Ghana TIN before setting up merchant accounts
   - ✅ Keep track of all income (in USD and GHS equivalent)
   - ✅ Save receipts for business expenses
   - ✅ Consult a Ghana tax professional or GRA
   - ✅ Consider setting aside funds for taxes
   - ✅ Check with your bank about international transfer fees
